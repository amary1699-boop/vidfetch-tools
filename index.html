Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about VidFetch Tools.

Which YouTube links are supported?

Standard YouTube watch links, youtu.be links, Shorts, Embed and Live URLs are supported.

Can I download the thumbnail?

Yes. After analyzing a supported YouTube URL, click Download Thumbnail.

Does Analyze download the video?

No. Analyze only identifies the YouTube video and loads its available thumbnail information.

Can I watch the video from VidFetch?

Yes. Click Watch Video after analyzing the URL. The video will open on YouTube.

Is VidFetch mobile friendly?

Yes. The interface is responsive for desktop, tablet and mobile devices.
